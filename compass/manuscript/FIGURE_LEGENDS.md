# Figure legends

## Additional global-ranking figure set

**Reaction ranking**, the complete rank-ordered BH-q curve for 6,533 variable reactions in Young 7 versus Older 6. The shaded CoQ interval includes all reactions tied with COQ3m_pos by raw p; it does not imply a uniquely resolved rank. The lower panel shows Cohen's d (Older minus Young), original p, BH q and the occupied raw-p rank interval for focal reactions. Blue points have reaction q < 0.05; grey points do not. All 10,211 model reactions, including 3,678 constants, remain in the source table.

**Subsystem dot plots**, all 90 testable RECON2 subsystems ordered by raw pathway p, shown over two pages. Dots indicate the older-minus-young mean donor rank-index difference; color encodes its sign and magnitude. Dot size is the fraction of variable constituent reactions with reaction-level q < 0.05. The right columns show the number of variable reactions, the separate 90-test pathway BH q, and the raw pathway-p rank interval. Exact ties may span the page boundary. Focal pathways are highlighted by label color, without excluding other subsystems. Transport, exchange and unassigned categories are included as model subsystems. Nine all-constant subsystems are retained only in the full table.

**Subsystem donor heatmap**, the same 90 subsystem indices across the 13 donors, with rows in pathway-p order and columns fixed by age and donor ID. Index values are mean within-reaction donor percentile ranks, ranging from zero to one. Each reaction was ranked across donors before aggregation; raw scores were not averaged across incompatible reaction scales. Colors do not represent metabolite concentrations or absolute flux. All new subsystem p values use exact donor-label permutations; pathway BH correction covers 90 tests, whereas reaction BH correction covers 6,533 tests.

All age comparisons shown here are unadjusted for sampling depth. Input-size sensitivity is presented in the depth-control figure.

## Figure 1. Age-associated metabolic reaction scores in human MuSCs

**A**, Reaction-level heatmap for the fixed BI13 roster (Young n = 7; Older n = 6), with age, cohort, Barthel Index (BI) and retained nucleus-count annotations. Values are z scores within each reaction across the 13 donors; the color scale is saturated at +/-2. BI values and nucleus counts are printed in the annotation tracks; grey BI cells indicate missing values, and nucleus-track shading represents log10 counts. Column order is fixed by age and donor ID, without outcome-based clustering. **B-G**, Individual donor COMPASS scores for COQ3m_pos (CoQ synthesis), ACITL_pos (ACLY), ACS_pos, CSm_pos (citrate synthase), ICDHyrm_pos (IDH2) and SUCD1m_pos (SDH). Blue, young; orange, older. Circles identify European donors and triangles Asian Chinese donors. Horizontal segments indicate group medians; horizontal offsets only separate overlapping points. Each donor contributes one biological replicate. Scores are -ln(1 + penalty); higher scores indicate greater predicted reaction potential. The printed p values are two-sided exact rank-label permutation tests; q values are BH-adjusted across the 6,533 nonconstant reactions in the age comparison. ACS denotes the AACS/ACSS2-associated model reaction. Metabolite concentrations and absolute fluxes were not measured.

## Figure 2. Age-stratified COQ8A associations with metabolic reaction scores

**A-D**, Donor COQ8A CPM versus CoQ synthesis, PDH, ACLY and SDH scores, respectively, in the expanded 21-donor roster. Young n = 7 and Older n = 14; symbols and age colors follow Fig. 1. The x axis uses log1p coordinates with ticks labelled in original CPM units; the rank correlation itself is unchanged by this monotonic transform. No regression line is imposed. Each point is one donor; exactly overlapping values can overlap visually. Spearman coefficients and two-sided permutation p values are printed separately for each age group. **E**, The complete eight-reaction focal panel, with color and top number encoding the Spearman coefficient and the second number giving its raw permutation p value. Young tests use 5,040 exact permutations; older tests use 49,999 random permutations with a plus-one correction. BH q values for the 72-method/scope comparisons are supplied in the full table, not substituted for the raw p labels shown here. The associations are unadjusted within age strata. Significant association in one age group and nonsignificant association in the other does not establish an age interaction.

## Figure S1. COQ8A high-low comparisons within each age group

**A-H**, The eight focal reaction scores in donor groups defined by within-age median COQ8A CPM. High is strictly above the median; low is at or below it. Young low n = 4 and high n = 3 (cutoff 108.133287 CPM); Older low n = 7 and high n = 7 (cutoff 33.387762 CPM). Y and O denote young and older. All donors are retained. Points, cohort symbols and median segments follow Fig. 1. The printed p values are two-sided exact rank-label permutation tests; q values use BH correction over all 16 comparisons. No comparison passed q < 0.05. These donor groups do not represent high-low nuclei paired within the same individual and are not independent replication of the continuous analysis.

## Figure S2. Network-wide age comparison and the complete ubiquinone synthesis subsystem

**A**, Reaction effect sizes (Cohen's d, Older minus Young) versus -log10(BH q) for all 6,533 variable reactions from the fixed BI13 comparison. Blue/orange points indicate q < 0.05 with lower/higher older-group scores, respectively; grey points indicate q >= 0.05. Each point is a directed model reaction; reactions can overlap. The horizontal reference marks q = 0.05. **B**, All 14 directed reactions assigned to Ubiquinone synthesis in the full model output. Within-reaction z scores and donor order follow Fig. 1; exact p and full-family q appear at right. All 14 reactions had identical donor rankings and therefore identical rank-test p values. This is network coupling, not 14 independent replications. Reaction formulas and associated genes are supplied in the full tables. COQ3m_pos produces ubiquinone-10 in the model annotation.

## Figure S3. Sensitivity of correlation and age-interaction estimates

**A**, Age-specific Spearman and Pearson coefficients, with each cell displaying coefficient / raw p value. Y, young (n = 7); O, older (n = 14). Pearson uses raw CPM or log1p CPM as labelled; Spearman p values are permutation-based and Pearson p values use the conventional two-sided test. **B**, All-donor (n = 21) Spearman/partial Spearman coefficients after no adjustment, adjustment for age group plus cohort, or additional adjustment for detected model-gene coverage. Cell p values use the approximate t reference distribution for all three columns to keep this panel's p method consistent. **C**, Older-minus-young interaction coefficients and 95% confidence intervals from raw-CPM OLS models with cohort adjustment and HC3 standard errors. COQ8A was standardized across all donors. Rows correspond to the adjacent reaction labels. Two-sided t p values appear at right; alternative scales, additional coverage adjustment and all q values are supplied in the tables. The data do not establish a statistically supported age-by-COQ8A interaction.

## Depth-control figure. Reaction scores under reduced input

Paired full-input and reduced-input COMPASS scores for CoQ, ACLY, PDH and SDH. Top: five-nucleus samples in nine eligible donors. Bottom: 10% UMI retention in all 13 donors. Lines connect each donor's complete-input score to the mean of three technical replicates; vertical segments show their range. Blue, young; orange, older. Every profile was independently normalized to CPM. Technical replicates contribute no additional biological sample size.
